About Us

Matrusri Engineering College, Saidabad, was established by Matrusri Education Society in 2011. The society was registered in 1980 with a group of far-sighted and distinguished engineers, doctors, lawyers, and administrators as its members, having a lofty vision and mission of imparting technical education of the highest standards among the students. The college offers courses in Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Information Technology, and Mechanical Engineering. The instructional facilities are spacious, and the laboratories are continuously upgraded with state-of-the-art equipment.

The college has a sophisticated Digital Library and also boasts an impressive repository of technical reference books and magazines catering to the needs of the students and faculty. Every effort is made to ensure that the students are trained in technical skills while inculcating in them a sense of social responsibility, in tune with the vision and mission of the college.
